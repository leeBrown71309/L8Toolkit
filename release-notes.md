L8 Toolkit 0.1.5 keeps covers and search results up to date on their own.

- **Covers always show up**: browsers (YouTube), YouTube Music and phone bridges often publish a track before its cover. The notch now asks again until the cover arrives, instead of keeping the music-note placeholder until a restart.
- **New apps are found right away**: install an app and search for it a few seconds later, no need to rebuild the index anymore. Microsoft Store apps appear the next time you open the notch.
- **New files too**: files added to your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music and Videos folders join the search shortly after, batched so downloads in progress never slow your PC down.
- **Live results**: if Search is open while the index refreshes, the results update without retyping.

Installed copies update from Settings › Updates, or from "Install update" in the tray menu.
