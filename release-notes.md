L8 Toolkit 0.1.3 stays out of the way of your games.

- **Pause in fullscreen apps**: while a game or a video fills the screen, the search shortcut (Alt+Space) and Quick Accent let every key through to it, so holding E and pressing Space in a game no longer opens the accent bar. Everything comes back as soon as you leave fullscreen. On by default, in Settings › General › Games and fullscreen.

Installed copies update from Settings › Updates, or from "Install update" in the tray menu.
