L8 Toolkit 0.1.2 brings Quick Accent, a livelier music player and a cleaner settings window.

- **Quick Accent**: hold a letter, press Space or an arrow, and its accented characters drop out of the notch. Move with the same keys, release the letter: the chosen character replaces it, in any app. 29 languages plus currencies (€ £ ¥…) and symbols (© ® ™ §…), set in Settings › Quick Accent, where you can also try it.
- **Music takes the colors of the cover**: the progress line is now a wave that moves while the track plays and settles flat on pause, and the whole widget follows the cover's color. Your accent color stays for Search and Quick Accent.
- **No more white flash** when the settings window opens, and it now always opens in front.

Installed copies update from Settings › Updates, or from "Install update" in the tray menu.
