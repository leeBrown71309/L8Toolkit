First public release of L8 Toolkit: small widgets in a virtual notch at the top of the screen.

- **Search**: apps, files, folders, the web and quick math, from `Alt+Space` or by resting the pointer on the notch. Run as administrator, show in folder, open with, copy, pin and more from the action menu (`Ctrl+K`).
- **Music**: whatever plays in Spotify, a browser or any media app, with its cover, a seekable progress bar and the controls. Phone bridges such as NectarLink and Phone Link are supported.
- **Live notch**: while media plays, the folded notch lights up along its edge with the track progress, and audio bars in its middle follow the sound.
- **Appearance**: dark, light or system theme, solid, translucent, Mica or Acrylic panels, and an accent color.
- **English and French** throughout.
- **Updates inside the app**: Settings › Updates checks GitHub, then downloads, verifies and installs signed updates in one click.
