L8 Toolkit 0.1.4 adds a timer and a history of what you copy, right in the notch.

- **Timer and Pomodoro**: start a countdown in one click (1 to 60 minutes) or a Pomodoro that chains focus and breaks on its own (25 / 5 / 15 minutes, 4 rounds, all adjustable in Settings › Timer). It keeps running with the notch closed, and a notification tells you when a period ends.
- **Music and timer together**: the activity you looked at last fills the notch, the other one waits in a small bubble beside it. Rest the pointer on the bubble to switch.
- **Recents**: your last copied texts, images, screenshots and files, kept after a restart (10 by default, up to 50). Click a card to copy it again, drag it into any app, or drop files on the notch to keep them at hand. A new screenshot shows briefly in the notch.
- **Password managers**: copies from Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePass and others are never shown in clear and stay encrypted on your PC. Settings › Recents can leave them out entirely.
- **Faster switching**: Alt+1 to Alt+4 jump to Search, Music, Timer and Recents, on any keyboard layout. Every Recents shortcut is listed in Settings › Recents.
- **Simpler settings**: theme, material and accent color now live in General.

Installed copies update from Settings › Updates, or from "Install update" in the tray menu.
